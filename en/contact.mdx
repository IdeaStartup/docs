## Details for an initial discussion

- Name, organisation and country.
- Your role: founder, organisation or technology provider.
- Stage: idea, prototype, live product or expansion.
- Problem and intended user.
- Required service: product, AI, integration, documentation or partnership.
- Budget and target timing, if defined.

## Before sharing information

A description is sufficient. Do not send passwords, account details, API keys or sensitive documents.

## Next step

A confirmed company name, business email and a privacy policy matching the actual enquiry workflow are required before activating contact.
