## Proposed collaboration models

- **Referrals and business development:** qualify opportunities under an agreed commercial arrangement.
- **Integration and delivery:** coordinate delivery teams, outputs and support.
- **Joint marketing:** local content, demonstrations and events with each party’s approval.

## Providers under evaluation

[Mambu partner programme](https://mambu.com/en/partner-programme) and [Salt Edge partner network](https://www.saltedge.com/company/partnerships).

We are assessing suitability and commercial models. No approved partnership or reseller status is represented in this draft.

## Commercial matters to clarify

Territory, products, opportunity registration, incentive and payment terms, contracting and support responsibilities, training, pricing and brand permissions.

## Technology collaboration with a defined outcome

We seek an institution to pilot file preparation and an implementation partner to support integration. Begin with one service and agreed acceptance measures, then evaluate results before an operating contract.

We evaluate Mambu for solutions, integration and referral routes subject to programme acceptance and agreement. We evaluate Mintlify Startups for knowledge and documentation support; acceptance is unconfirmed. Using Mintlify or Cloudflare does not confer certified partner status.
