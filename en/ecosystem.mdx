The first edition contains **100 startups and scaleups** and **100 funding, investment and support organisations**. These include venture capital firms, angel networks, banks, accelerators, incubators and government bodies.

[Open the interactive directory in English](https://app.innofinhub.com/ecosystem?lang=en)

## What is included?

- Search by name, sector or founder, and filter by record type, location and contact availability.
- Company profiles, available founder names, published contact channels and their sources.
- Funding/support roles, instruments and eligibility notes.
- 52 published historical funding-round records, with sources and announcement dates where available.
- Filtered CSV export and the original Arabic Excel workbook.

**Sources reviewed on 22 October 2026.** Some addresses serve support, sales or media teams. Each record identifies the channel purpose and evidence. Publication does not establish current deliverability or a response from the organisation.

## Outreach and verification

Current fundraising needs and application windows are **unconfirmed**. A past round does not establish an open raise today. Some records need confirmation of their current UAE presence. Review notes and evidence before matching or outreach.

Outreach starts at “Not contacted”. Changes are saved **only in your browser**, do not sync with your team and send no messages. CSV includes this browser’s statuses; Excel retains the source snapshot. Record descriptions retain their original Arabic in both interfaces.

Listing does not imply an IFH partnership or endorsement. Some organisations provide incubation or investor introductions without direct funding. Different channels may belong to the same corporate group.
