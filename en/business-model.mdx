## From an incomplete application to a review-ready file

We propose an Arabic/English portal that gathers company information and documents, checks completeness and consistency, and prepares a clear file for staff review. Admission and financing decisions remain with the institution.

<Note>This is a proposed service under development. The introductory portal is available; application intake, document processing and automated execution are not yet activated.</Note>

## Who we serve

| Institutional customer | Proposed use |
| --- | --- |
| Incubators and business service centres | Prepare applications to support programmes |
| SME programme operators | Organise files, missing information and follow-up |
| Suitable financial institutions | Prepare financing files to their requirements, subject to agreement and review |

The institution pays for the service. Applicant companies and reviewing teams use the portal.

## First product

An incubator application: receive company information, index documents, flag missing or inconsistent information, prepare a reviewer brief and draft follow-up for staff approval. Start with one service and a customer-approved requirements list.

## Commercial model

| Stage | Deliverable | Proposed charge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Limited pilot | Configure and test a service; report results | Defined pilot fee |
| Setup and integration | Institutional branding, requirements, access and integration | Implementation fee |
| Ongoing operation | Support, maintenance, hosting and agreed usage | Monthly or annual subscription |
| Expansion | Additional services, integrations or applications | Separate scope or usage charge |

Pricing depends on volume, document complexity and integration. No fixed prices or referral commissions are approved. Vendor subscriptions and our service fees are separated in proposals.

## Prove the value

An initial pilot may use 30–50 synthetic or authorised files, compared with a baseline for the same service. Measure file preparation time, data extraction and missing-item detection accuracy, staff corrections, follow-up rounds and cost per completed file. This sample is proposed for early learning, not statistical proof or a promised saving.

## Expansion

After validating the first service, explore Islamic financing file preparation with a suitable institution. Integration depends on its existing system interfaces; Mambu is not required.
