We help teams assess technology for Islamic financial products and operations, with a clear user experience.

## Areas to assess

- Systems supporting Islamic accounts and financing.
- Data, reporting and liquidity management.
- Process documentation and customer journeys.
- Integration with external services and systems.

## Providers for evaluation

[Mambu](https://mambu.com/en/islamic-banking) · [Azentio iMAL](https://www.azentio.com/banking/imal-islamic-banking) · [Temenos](https://www.temenos.com/products/islamic-banking/) · [Finastra](https://www.finastra.com/universal-banking/islamic-finance)

These are research links, not approved partnerships or endorsements of individual products.

<Note>Technology support does not replace Shariah approval of the product and contracts, or verification of the applicable regulatory requirements.</Note>

## Future remittance pathway

A digital remittance journey is a future objective, subject to a suitable partner, confirmed corridors, costs and requirements. This portal does not offer money transfers.

## Later pathway: equipment financing file preparation

After validating application preparation, explore gathering company information, an equipment quotation and documents to a suitable financial institution's requirements. The assistant identifies missing information and prepares a brief; the institution retains customer and financing approval, compliance, Sharia governance and financial execution.

Mambu is a technology provider under evaluation, not the funding bank. There is no contracted financial institution or live financing service through this portal. Activity scope, marketing, referrals and data handling require review before operation.
