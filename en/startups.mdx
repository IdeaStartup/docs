## Who is it for?

Founders testing an idea, teams with a prototype, and startups seeking technology partners or a growth plan.

| Stage | Key question | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Who needs this and why? | Hypotheses and user interviews |
| Product design | What is essential? | User journey and first-version scope |
| Prototype testing | Does it create value? | Prototype and actionable feedback |
| Launch preparation | What will delivery require? | Resources, providers and measures |
| Partnership preparation | How do we present the opportunity? | Pitch and an assumption-based financial model |

## Opportunities to assess

A knowledge assistant, SME cash-flow dashboard, reporting automation or a customer journey for a service delivered by a suitable financial partner.

Preparation can clarify funding needs and improve the project brief. Investment and partnership decisions remain with the relevant organisations.
