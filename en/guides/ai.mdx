## Example: a customer-service knowledge assistant

**Inputs:** approved guides and FAQs. **Outputs:** an answer with a source, or a handoff when information is missing.

| Test dimension | Measure |
| --- | --- |
| Answer quality | Accuracy and source alignment |
| Time | Time to a useful answer |
| Missing information | Clarification or appropriate handoff |
| Cost | Cost per successful request and review |

## Pilot boundaries

Start with public content and synthetic data. The initial pilot does not authorise transfers or independent financial decisions.

## Moving to production

Review results with users, define human review, knowledge sources and maintenance before scaling.

This portal describes a proposed service and does not include an operational AI assistant yet.
