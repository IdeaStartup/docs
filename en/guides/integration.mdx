This is a planning guide, not documentation for a live company API.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the use case">Document users, essential functions, expected volume and outcome.</Step>
  <Step title="Map data and permissions">Identify sources, permissions, necessary fields and retention boundaries.</Step>
  <Step title="Select a provider">Check geography, banks, currencies, prices, requirements and support.</Step>
  <Step title="Run a bounded pilot">Use a sandbox and synthetic data, then test success and failure scenarios.</Step>
  <Step title="Agree operations">Assign monitoring, error handling, updates and support before launch.</Step>
</Steps>
